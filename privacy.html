Limitless Innovations Ltd

Privacy notice

Last updated 29 September 2026

This notice explains what personal information Limitless Innovations Ltd collects through limitlessinnovations.co.uk, why, how long we keep it and what your rights are. We collect only what we need to answer enquiries and supply orders. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't sell your information.

1. Who we are

Limitless Innovations Ltd is the controller of your personal information. We're a private limited company registered in England and Wales, company number 14380770, with our registered office at Unit A, 82 James Carter Road, Mildenhall, Suffolk, IP28 7DE.

For questions about this notice or your information, email info@limitlessinnovations.co.uk. We haven't appointed a Data Protection Officer, as we're not required to.

2. What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, phone number (optional), your message, and which page and form you used.
  • Trade order enquiries: company name, contact name, email address, phone number (optional), VAT number (optional), delivery postcode (optional), notes, and the products, quantities and prices shown when you sent it.
  • Orders and invoices: the details above, any delivery details you give us, the invoice and whether it has been paid.
  • Emails: your email address and what you send us.
  • Technical information: your IP address and the date and time of each message or enquiry. When you visit the site, Cloudflare, our host, processes IP addresses and request logs.

The forms show which details are required. Without them we can't reply to you or deal with your order. We never receive your card details (see section 4).

3. Why we use it and our lawful basis

Purpose Lawful basis
Replying to messages and enquiries Legitimate interests: responding to people who contact us
Handling order enquiries, invoices, payment and delivery Contract, if you buy as an individual or sole trader; otherwise legitimate interests: dealing with business customers and their staff

You can object to any use based on legitimate interests (see section 8).

4. Who we share it with

  • Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the website and the database (Cloudflare D1) that holds messages and enquiries, and protects the site. It processes IP addresses and request logs to do this.
  • Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc.) emails us an alert with each new message or order. Alerts are sent from its EU region (Ireland), but Resend is based in the US and stores message content and logs there.
  • Proton AG hosts our email, including the alerts and any emails between us.
  • Stripe (Stripe Payments UK Ltd and Stripe Payments Europe Ltd) handles payment when we send you a Stripe invoice. We give Stripe your company name, contact name, email address, phone number, VAT number and delivery postcode (where given) and the invoice lines. Card details go straight to Stripe, never to our website. Stripe also uses some information for its own purposes, such as preventing fraud; see stripe.com/gb/privacy.
  • Suppliers, distributors and couriers receive the delivery address and contact details needed to deliver your order, as goods usually come straight from our supplier.

We also share information when the law requires it, for example with HMRC.

5. Transfers outside the UK

Cloudflare, Resend and Stripe may process information in the US and other countries, and Proton is based in Switzerland. These transfers are protected either by UK adequacy regulations, which cover Switzerland, the EEA and US organisations certified under the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (the "UK-US data bridge"), or by contract clauses approved for UK transfers: the International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Ask us if you'd like details.

7. Cookies and browser storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and our fonts come from our own website, not Google Fonts or any other font service. We use:

  • Trade basket (your browser's local storage): remembers the products you've added until you send your enquiry or clear the basket. It stays on your device.
  • Chat assistant (your browser's session storage): keeps your conversation until you close the browser tab. The assistant answers from text built into the website; what you type isn't sent to us or anyone else.
  • li_desk (cookie): keeps you logged in to our private pages, such as our order desk. It's set only if you log in with a password and lasts up to 30 days, or until you log out.

The basket, chat and login storage are strictly necessary for features you choose to use, so they don't need your consent. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

Visit counts. Our server counts page visits by day, country, device type (phone, tablet or computer, worked out from what your browser sends with each request) and the website you came from. This uses no cookies or other storage on your device, we don't keep your IP address for it, and the daily totals can't identify you.

8. Your rights

You have the right to get a copy of your personal information, have it corrected, have it deleted, restrict how we use it, object to uses based on legitimate interests, and receive information you gave us for a contract in a portable format. Some rights have limits: for example, we must keep invoices the law requires us to keep.

To use your rights, email us. We'll reply within one month, or tell you within that month if a complex request needs up to two more. There's normally no charge, and we may ask you to confirm your identity.

9. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your personal information, please tell us first at info@limitlessinnovations.co.uk. We'll acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, look into it and tell you the outcome.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

10. Changes to this notice

We'll update this notice if the way we use personal information changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
