LIMITLESS INNOVATIONS WEBSITE - HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE

This folder is the whole website. It needs no server, database or code changes.

QUICKEST (about 10 minutes, free):
1. Create a free Cloudflare account at dash.cloudflare.com and turn on two-step login.
2. Go to Workers & Pages > Create application > Get started > Drag and drop your files.
3. Name the project (e.g. limitless-innovations), drag in THIS FOLDER (the one containing index.html)
   and select Deploy site.
4. Cloudflare gives you a free web address ending in .pages.dev. The site is live and uses https.

YOUR OWN ADDRESS (e.g. limitlessinnovations.co.uk):
5. Buy the domain from a registrar, then connect it:
   Workers & Pages > your project > Custom domains > Set up a custom domain.
   Cloudflare shows exactly which settings to change at your registrar.

TO UPDATE THE SITE LATER:
- Edit the files, then upload the folder again as a new deployment. Old versions are kept and can be restored.

BEFORE GOING LIVE, FILL IN (search index.html for these):
- [company number] and [Registered office address]  (legally required on UK company websites)
- [product] placeholders in the materials lists, if you want exact products shown

THE CHAT BUBBLE ("Warden"):
- Works straight away with instant answers. To switch on real AI answers, follow
  chat-assistant/CHAT-ASSISTANT-SETUP.txt (about 15 minutes, needs an Anthropic account).

THE SHOP:
- The trade basket sends an order enquiry by email. See SHOP-SETUP-NOTES.txt for what a
  full online shop with payment would need.

The file _headers adds security settings. Leave it in the folder.
