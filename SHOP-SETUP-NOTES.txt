LIMITLESS INNOVATIONS WEBSITE — SHOP AND PROJECT SETUP NOTES

PURPOSE
This is a one-page website prototype for fire-protection services and trade materials. The intended experience is to browse services, installation examples and products in one place, check product details without opening a separate page, collect quantities in a basket and continue to an order step.

FILES
- index.html: the complete website page and current interactions.
- images/products: product photographs used in the materials catalogue.
- images: other website image assets.
- _headers: hosting security headers. Keep this file when deploying to a compatible static host.
- HOW-TO-PUT-ONLINE.txt: deployment outline and the limits of the current static version.
- Limitless-Innovations-website-preview.html: a standalone preview file supplied alongside the website folder in the redesign package.

CURRENT SHOP BEHAVIOUR
- Visitors can browse the product catalogue and filter by manufacturer.
- They can add products and quantities to the trade basket.
- The basket is saved in that browser on that device. It is not yet shared between browsers or devices.
- The basket form gathers business and contact information and opens an email draft addressed to the configured site email.
- This is an order enquiry only. The site does not currently take payment, verify live availability, calculate VAT or delivery, create a confirmed order, or send an automatic receipt.
- An email draft depends on the visitor having a working email application configured. It is not a reliable substitute for a production order service.

BEFORE PUBLISHING THE CURRENT INFORMATION SITE
1. Replace the registered-office and company-number placeholders in index.html with verified company information.
2. Confirm the public contact email, phone number, address and all social links.
3. Review every service statement, case study, product description, rating, application and technical document link.
4. Confirm that the product photographs are approved for this commercial use and keep any required credits.
5. Add current privacy, cookie, terms, delivery and returns information appropriate to the business.
6. Open the site on a phone and desktop. Check navigation, product filters, basket quantities, empty-basket messages and the email enquiry handoff.
7. Do not describe the email enquiry as a completed purchase.

TO SELL PRODUCTS DIRECTLY TO OTHER COMPANIES
A static website upload alone does not create a functioning online shop. Before taking orders or payments, decide and configure:

- Product catalogue: product codes, approved names, brands, pack sizes, variants, images, technical documents and confirmed prices.
- Trade rules: customer eligibility, any account pricing or minimum quantities, purchase-order references and accepted payment methods.
- Fulfilment: stock or lead times, delivery zones, shipping rates, delivery addresses and order tracking or status messages.
- Tax and sales terms: applicable VAT/tax rules, invoices, returns, privacy information and sales terms. Confirm these for the company’s actual markets.
- Order system: a commerce service or backend that securely records orders, handles stock and sends confirmations.
- Payment: use a reputable payment provider for card payments. Do not collect or store card details in this static page.
- Support: decide who receives new orders and handles payment, delivery and product questions.

The recommended customer flow remains on this page for catalogue search, product detail and basket review. A secure hosted payment step may open separately when the customer pays. For reliable orders, replace the email-draft handoff with the chosen commerce service and test the complete flow before launch.

ONE-PAGE PERFORMANCE NOTES
- Keep product detail in an in-page panel or expandable section rather than requiring a new page for every item.
- Use small responsive product thumbnails, load images below the first screen only as they approach view, and reserve their display dimensions to prevent layout shifts.
- Keep the first view light and make the basket shortcut easy to reach.
- A one-page design does not need to load every full-size photo and optional feature immediately.

DEPLOYMENT
The current information and enquiry prototype can be hosted as static files. Follow HOW-TO-PUT-ONLINE.txt and upload the folder containing index.html and its supporting assets. This only publishes the current prototype; it does not switch on direct payment or order management. Complete the direct-sales setup above before advertising a paid online checkout.

SITE CONTACT SETTING
The current order-enquiry email is configured in index.html in the SITE settings. Change it there if the sales inbox changes, then verify both the footer contact link and the basket enquiry on a device with an email application configured.
