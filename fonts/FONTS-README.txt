Fonts used by the private desk (self-hosted, so no outside font service is called).

Source Serif 4  (400, 500, 600)   https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-serif   licence: OFL-SourceSerif4.txt
IBM Plex Mono   (400, 500)        https://github.com/IBM/plex              licence: OFL-IBMPlexMono.txt

Both are SIL Open Font License 1.1. The .woff2 files are the Latin subset (letters, digits, punctuation, GBP and
euro signs, arrows) cut from the Google Fonts releases of each family; Source Serif 4 is fixed at optical size 20.
archivo.woff2 and inter.woff2 are the public website's fonts and are used for the printable invoice.
